# Grow log gallery and photos
## Requirements
* Users can add multiple photos to an entry and remove them.
* Deleting an entry also deletes its photos.
* Users can browse a grow log’s photos at their original quality.

## User scenarios
### 1. A user uploads photos to a report
1. Open a grow log and select multiple photos in the report form.
2. Save the report.
3. Verify uploaded photos appear in the form, entry history, and gallery, and any upload failures are reported.

### 2. A user removes a photo from an entry
1. Open today’s report or select Edit beside another entry.
2. Remove a photo.
3. Verify it no longer appears in the form, entry history, or gallery.

### 3. A user deletes an entry and its photos
1. Delete an entry with photos and confirm.
2. Verify the entry and its photos no longer appear in the history or gallery.

### 4. A user browses a grow log’s photos
1. Open a mustard grow started on 15 October 2026, with bloom starting on 16 November and photos on 19 and 20 November.
2. Select the 20 November photo.
3. Verify the full-screen viewer shows `20 Nov 2026 · Day 37 · Week 6 · bloom 5`.
4. Press the right arrow key to navigate to the 19 November photo.
5. Verify the viewer shows `19 Nov 2026 · Day 36 · Week 6 · bloom 4`.
6. Press the left arrow key and verify the 20 November photo appears.
7. Press Escape and verify the viewer closes.
